Tokens:

<integer> ::= [0-9]+
<double>  ::= [0-9]+ '.' [0-9]+
<plus>    ::= '+'
<minus>   ::= '-'
<mult>    ::= '*'
<divide>  ::= '/'
<num>     ::= <integer> | <double>
<op>      ::= <plus> | <minus> | <mult> | <divide>


Grammar:

<expr> ::= <expr> <op> <num>
         | <num>
<num>  ::= <integer> | <double>
<op>   ::= '+' | '-'

<expr> ::= <expr> ('+' | '-') <term>
         | <term>
<term> ::= <term>  ('*' | '/') <num>
        | num
<num>  ::= <integer> | <double>
<op>   ::= '+' | '-'



<expr> ::= <expr> (<plus>|<minus>) <term>
        |  <term>
<term> ::= <term> (<mult>|<divide>) <factor>
        | <factor>
<factor> ::= ( <expr> )
         | <num>
<num>  ::= <integer> | <double>
<op>   ::= '+' | '-'| '*' | '/'



REMOVE LEFT RECURSION



<expr> ::= <term> <expr'>
<expr'> ::= ('+' | '-') <term>
          | e

<term> ::= <factor> <term'>
<term'> ::= (<mult>|<divide>) <factor>
          | e

<factor> ::= ( <expr> )
         | <num>


<num>  ::= <integer> | <double>
<op>   ::= '+' | '-'| '*' | '/'
